# Enerchain Economics

## Fundamental Assumption

Energy is the foundation of productive work.

Every advanced economic activity ultimately consumes energy.

---

## Economic Model

Enerchain proposes that energy generation creates newly issued currency.

Participants contribute value to society through energy production and receive protocol rewards.

---

## Potential Contributors

- Residential Solar
- Community Solar
- Wind Farms
- Hydroelectric Facilities
- Nuclear Facilities
- Fusion Systems
- Future Planetary Infrastructure

---

## Economic Benefits

Potential advantages include:

- Incentivized energy generation
- Strong link between production and issuance
- Transparent accounting
- Global participation
- Reduced geographic monetary asymmetry

---

## Risks

- Oversupply of generation
- Hardware centralization
- Regulatory interference
- Market manipulation
- Uneven energy access

These risks require additional research.
