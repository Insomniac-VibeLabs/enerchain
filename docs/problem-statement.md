# Problem statement

Edition: doc-0.3 (2026-10-08).

## Plain language

Money is not denominated in the thing the technical economy runs on. Electricity is that thing. Mining a proof-of-work coin already spends it. Putting power on the grid does not, today, mint a public unit. Enerchain is the proposal that it should, and that the unit should be what regions — and later planets — trade.

## Technical statement

Energy scarcity constrains economic growth more tightly than energy abundance, and electricity is the high-quality carrier modern economies shift toward [Stern 2011]. Proof-of-work settlement already makes electricity the operating cost of a public ledger [de Vries 2018]. Distributed agents can inject as well as withdraw; integrating them is a market-design task [Parag and Sovacool 2016]. Interconnected grids already exchange power across regions, at prices that differ by location [Schweppe et al. 1988; Hogan 1992].

The missing instrument is a hardware-locked, transferable unit whose issue size is the measured injection. Enerchain is that instrument. The long-range claim is that the region in “regional trade” scales to a planet and then a solar system without changing the unit.
