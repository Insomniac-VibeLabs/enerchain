# Problem Statement

Modern economies rely on increasingly abstract representations of value.

At the same time, civilization's dependence on electrical energy continues to grow.

Electricity powers:

- AI systems
- Data centers
- Factories
- Transportation
- Communications
- Scientific research

Despite this dependence, direct energy generation is rarely represented as a native digital economic asset.

Enerchain explores whether verified production of electrical energy can become the basis for a decentralized currency system.

The central question is:

Can value creation be tied directly to energy creation?
