# Circuit sheets

Edition: doc-1.4 (2026-10-10). EC-SEAL1 revision B. Sheets 01–08 and [mint-path.svg](mint-path.svg) are generated by [tools/gen_schematics.py](../../tools/gen_schematics.py) from the part values in [CIRCUITS.md](../../hardware/fab/ec-seal1/CIRCUITS.md). The script recomputes every number printed on a sheet and stops if it disagrees with the circuit calculations, and it checks every connection a sheet draws against [netlist.txt](../../hardware/fab/ec-seal1/netlist.txt). CI regenerates them and fails on any difference.

Symbols follow IEC 60617: a resistor is a rectangle, a capacitor is two plates, a fuse is a rectangle with a line through it, an inductor is a row of arcs, a diode is a triangle and bar, ground is the stacked earth mark. Ground on this board is Line at the grid side of the shunt. These are design drawings, not fabrication artwork; the order is [hardware/fab/ec-seal1/](../../hardware/fab/ec-seal1/MANUFACTURER.md).

The doc-0.7 sheets (X2 dropper, 0.5 V divider, active-low tamper, a schedule in the secure element) are in the git history before doc-1.3. Their mistakes are the subject of [electrical-review.md](../electrical-review.md).
