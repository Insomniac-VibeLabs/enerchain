# Enerchain

## Power is the new gold.

Enerchain is a proposed distributed energy currency protocol that rewards verified electrical energy generation through a blockchain-based settlement network.

Unlike traditional cryptocurrencies that derive value from computational work and energy consumption, Enerchain uses Proof of Generation (PoG) to reward the creation and delivery of useful electrical energy.

The project's long-term goal is to explore whether energy generation can become a globally transferable economic primitive for an increasingly electrified civilization.

---

## Core Thesis

Modern civilization depends on:

- Electricity
- Computation
- Artificial Intelligence
- Manufacturing
- Communications
- Transportation

All ultimately depend on energy.

Enerchain proposes that verified energy generation may represent a more fundamental basis for economic value than fiat issuance or computational work.

---

## How It Works

1. Energy is generated.
2. Energy contribution is measured by certified hardware.
3. Measurements are cryptographically signed.
4. The Enerchain network verifies the contribution.
5. Tokens are minted according to protocol rules.
6. Tokens become transferable on a public ledger.

---

## Why Enerchain?

Bitcoin converts electricity consumption into monetary value.

Enerchain explores the opposite approach:

> What if producing useful energy became the mining process?

## Design Goals

- Decentralized participation
- Public verification
- Non-repudiation
- Hardware-based trust
- Resistance to fraud
- Global scalability
- Interplanetary compatibility

---

## Example

A home solar installation contributes:

10 kWh

The Enerchain Meter records:

- Voltage
- Current
- Power Factor
- Time
- Delivered Energy
- Meter Identity

The measurement is signed and submitted to the network.

Validators verify the contribution and issue protocol rewards.
